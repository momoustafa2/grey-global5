Custom orders

Built to your spec, in five steps

Need something the catalog doesn't have yet? Custom collection follows the same pipeline every time, in any language, from a single locale to a global rollout.

STEP / 01

Spec

You define language, modality, volume, and format. We scope contributors and timeline.

STEP / 02

Recruit

We source native contributors in the target locale, screened and consented.

STEP / 03

Capture

Recording, photography, or filming happens in-field or in-studio, to your protocol.

STEP / 04

Review

Native-language QA, transcription, and labeling pass before anything ships.

STEP / 05

Deliver

Data lands in your format and schema, with an audit trail for every file.
